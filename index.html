Strategic Gaps — ABSA's Unfair Advantages
Gap 01 — Measurement Infrastructure

No competitor in Mauritius is running Meta Conversions API (CAPI). All competitors have weak conversion tracking and unattributed ad spend. ABSA can gain a significant signal quality advantage — lower CPAs and better optimisation — within 2 weeks of CAPI activation.

Gap 02 — Entrepreneur Segment

Spark by Absa is the only dedicated entrepreneur banking brand in Mauritius — yet the segment is entirely uncontested creatively. No competitor is running targeted entrepreneur/SME banking creative on Meta. A focused Spark campaign would own this audience with minimal competition.

Gap 03 — Multi-Currency Virtual Card

ABSA launched Mauritius's first multi-currency virtual card in March 2026 — a genuinely unique product feature. This is massively undermarketed relative to product quality. Digital professionals and frequent travellers are an untapped, high-intent audience with zero competitor overlap.
