Key Recommendations
  1. Fix Reliability First: App crash sentiment (2.9★ vs 3.9★ for Spark) is a credibility leak. Product fix must precede major acquisition campaigns. This is table stakes.
  2. Spark Business Dominance: 220+ merchants + 65K transactions is early validation. Allocate 20-25% of budget to SME/entrepreneur messaging on LinkedIn + Google (B2B). This is differentiation vs MCB/SBM.
  3. Payments Narrative Wins: Absa is strongest in payments (729K Facebook engagement, Google Pay + QR leadership). Lead with "Your Payments, Simplified" for consumer. Test this messaging hard.
  4. Content Gap — Mortgages & Business Banking: Current content shows 38K views (mortgages) and 108K (business). Underinvested vs opportunity. Create dedicated mortgage + SME content series (YouTube + Blog).
  5. Organic Search Authority: Build SEO dominance on payments, digital banking, Spark keywords. This is lower-cost, high-trust acquisition for consideration stage.
